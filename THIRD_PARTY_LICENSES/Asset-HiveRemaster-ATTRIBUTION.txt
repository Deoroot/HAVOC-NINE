CREDITS for h9_tsrp_hive_rm (Hive City remaster)
================================================
The remaster keeps the layout, entities and gameplay of h9_tsrp_hive (Alexandria, Paradise City and Xenon City combined).
Everything new in it was made with SMAK (the studio maker asset kit): the models in ts/models/hr, the textures in
hr_hive.wad, and the PrimeXT normal/gloss maps (ts/textures/hr_*_norm.tga, _gloss.tga) and material scripts
(ts/scripts/hr_c*.mat). The textures are Poly Haven materials (CC0 1.0, https://polyhaven.com, no attribution required;
credited here gladly), re-toned and cut to 256 colours, or hive v1's own textures redrawn (credited with h9_tsrp_hive and
its source maps: alexandria_msr_b5 / paradisecity_revival / xenoncity_true_b3v2 and their wads; see creds_attribution.txt).
The furniture and props that are not in ts/models/hr are the ones h9_tsrp_hive already uses.

This file is the merge of the per-zone CREDITS.txt files (zones c02 to c27), c01-mat/out/CREDITS.txt and the c01 props
(c01-props, SMAK models hr_bin, hr_box, hr_box_open, hr_bulklamp, hr_conduit, hr_conduit2, hr_junction).

c01 props: skins from Poly Haven (CC0 1.0; https://polyhaven.com/a/<name>):
